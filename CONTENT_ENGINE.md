# Lumi — Faceless Content Engine (the part that actually makes the money)

The app is the easy part. **Installs come from content.** Cal AI didn't win on tech — it won on TikTok volume.
You already run faceless content pipelines, so this is your edge. Plug Lumi into it.

## The one-line strategy
Post **3–5 short videos/day** across TikTok + Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts, every one ending on the same CTA: *"Your skin's free Glow Score is in Lumi."* Volume + a proven hook > production polish.

## The 4 formats that print (skincare is the most viral niche on TikTok)
1. **Score reveal / reaction** — screen-record a selfie → scanning animation → Glow Score. "I let an AI rate my skin and I was NOT ready." (Your highest-retention format — it IS the product.)
2. **Glow-up before/after** — 4-week transformation, score 61 → 79. "I followed what the app told me for 30 days."
3. **Myth / mistake** — "You're wasting money on vitamin C if your skin scores low on THIS." → tease the metric → CTA.
4. **Niche targeting** — one video each for: men's skincare, teen acne, 30s anti-aging, "skincare on a budget." Same app, different audience hook. Niche videos out-convert generic ones.

## 10 hooks ready to shoot (first 1.5 seconds = everything)
1. "I scanned my skin with AI and the score was brutal."
2. "POV: an app just told you why your skin looks tired."
3. "Your $0 skincare upgrade starts with one selfie."
4. "Dermatologist booked out 3 months. This took 10 seconds."
5. "Men: you have a 4-step routine you're skipping."
6. "I tracked my Glow Score for 30 days. Here's the graph."
7. "Stop buying serums until you know your skin's actual problem."
8. "The AI found redness I couldn't even see."
9. "Rate my skin… or let the AI do it." (duet/stitch bait)
10. "This is why your skin looks dull in photos."

## Script template (15–20s)
```
[0:00] HOOK (one of the 10 above) — on screen as bold caption
[0:02] Show the selfie → tap analyze → scanning animation (real screen recording)
[0:06] Score reveal — react. Read 2 metrics out loud ("texture 58… ouch")
[0:11] "It built me this exact routine" — flash the AM/PM screen
[0:15] CTA: "Get your free Glow Score — link in bio. App's called Lumi."
```

## Production via your existing pipeline
- **Screen recordings** of the real app = your best B-roll. Record 20 once, reuse forever.
- **Voiceover:** ElevenLabs (you already have Brian + others) for faceless narration.
- **Faces:** AI avatars / UGC-style or pure screen-record-over-voiceover (no face needed).
- **Editing:** same captioned-vertical pipeline you run for the channels.
- **Batch:** shoot a week (25 videos) in one session, schedule daily.

## The funnel (where the money is made)
```
TikTok/Reels/Shorts hook
   → "free Glow Score, link in bio"
   → App Store page (the ASO + screenshots do the selling)
   → Free scan + score (the aha moment)
   → Paywall at "Get my routine" → 3-day trial → $6.99/wk
```
Every weak point is fixable: bad hook → swap it; people install but don't subscribe → tune the paywall copy/price; people don't install → fix the App Store screenshots.

## What to measure (and the honest targets)
- **Hook rate** (3-sec view %) — aim >45%; below 30% kill the hook.
- **Install rate** from App Store page view — aim >30% (good screenshots).
- **Trial start %** of free scanners — aim >5–8%.
- **Trial → paid %** — aim >35% (weekly-trial benchmark).
- LTV target ≈ $40–55 (the weekly-trial config benchmark). Keep CAC (mostly your time) well under it.

## Reality check
Most videos flop. The model is: post 100, ~5 pop, 1 goes big and carries the week. That's normal — it's a volume game, and volume is exactly what your pipeline is built for. **The single thing that determines if Lumi makes money is whether you post daily for 60+ days.** Not the code.
